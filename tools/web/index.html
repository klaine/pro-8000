How it worksSound design

Drag a knob up and down (shift: fine), or click it and use the scroll wheel or arrow keys; double-click resets it. The page follows the synth about once a second, and moving a control here puts that knob in pickup on the panel, as loading a patch does. Switches show the panel buttons as the sound uses them; a click holds the state until you press that button on the panel. Knobs, depth bars and the mod matrix edit the layer you're editing (Layer 1 / 2 at the top). Amber knobs are shared by both layers.

Everything this firmware adds, reached from the synth itself. Buttons are as printed on the panel; codes are what the display shows. Web-only features are listed at the end.

The display

  • A knob shows its value while turned, in real units: FREQ A/B follow their mode (semitones, octaves or Hz for Fixed), cents (FINE), −99..99 (M TUNE, Macro 1), Hz with a point from 1 kHz (CUTOFF), ms / s (envelopes, GLIDE), divisions when synced (LFO FREQ). PW A / B: 5-95 % of the cycle, 50 a square.
  • No plus sign: positive values have none.
  • A dot after the value = the knob hasn't picked up yet. Turn it past (or onto) the stored value and it takes over. Happens after loading a patch, switching layer / LFO / envelope, or editing from the web page. With pickup off (Shift + FIL ENV AMT P.UP OFF) the knob takes over as soon as you move it: the value jumps to it.

Patches

  • Load: in preset mode, type the number (1-128; a third digit or a pause ends it), or VALUE steps through them (P 7, P128). An empty slot loads the init sound.
  • Save: Rec, two digits or VALUE, Rec. Preset cancels.
  • Init: Perf, then Rec to keep it anywhere.
  • A patch keeps both layers, settings, routes, LFO shapes and step patterns, and its sequence. M VOL isn't stored.

Layers

  1. Settings 3 LAYr, VALUE: OFF SPLt StAC CHAn (MIDI channel 2 = layer 2) 8CH (a voice per channel 1-8) nPE (MPE: channel 1 master, 2-9 a voice each).
  2. Voices per layer: on that page Arp Up-Dn Lr.Uo, VALUE: AUto (4 + 4, 7 + 1 with one layer mono) or 1-7 … 7-1 (layer 1 - layer 2).
  3. Split point: Settings 4, then VALUE or play the note.
  4. Seq 1 / Seq 2 choose the layer you edit.

Shared by both layers: mixer, noise, resonance, PW A/B, PMOD OSC B, M VOL, every button.

LFOs

  1. Arp Up-Dn picks LFO 1 / LFO 2 (LED lit = 2).
  2. LFO Shape: trI PULS SA.UP SA.dn SInE S-H drFt StEP nOIS
  3. LFO 2 routing: select it, press LFO → Freq / Filter / PW Fr.on FL.oF. The button positions keep showing LFO 1.
  4. Sync: Settings 1 SYnC/SYn2 → FrEE CLoC. With the clock, LFO FREQ picks 8bAr … 1-32.
  5. Retrigger: Settings 2 trIG/trI2 → FrEE 1St EACH PoLY (per voice).

The mod wheel adds LFO 1 depth. The StEP pattern is edited on the web page.

Envelopes

  1. Shift (Sync Source) + the filter ADSR knobs edit Env 3, + the amp ADSR knobs Env 4 (with pickup). The keyboard-tracking switch is keyboard tracking.
  2. Env 3 / 4 do nothing until a mod route uses them (sources 7, 8).
  3. Keytracking itself: Settings 5 trAC → OFF HALF FULL.
  4. Mode: Settings 6 En.A En.F En.3 En.4 (6 again: next), VALUE: AdSr dAHd, cycling CYCL or a division (synced).
  5. Mode: Settings 6 En.A En.F En.3 En.4 (6 again: next), VALUE: AdSr dAHd, from key up UP.AS UP.dA, cycling CYCL or a division (synced).
  6. Trigger: on that page Arp Up-Dn tr.A…, VALUE: dn (key down) UP (key up), in any mode.
  7. On that page ATT sets the delay dLY and DEC the hold HoLd (DAHDSR); the knobs leave the attack and decay alone meanwhile.
  8. Timing: Arp Up-Dn again tI.A…, VALUE: SEC or SYnC (the time knobs, delay and hold step through 0, 1/32 … 8 bars at the tempo).
  9. Curve: Arp Up-Dn again CU.A…, VALUE: AnLG LIn SUEL.
  10. Curve: on that page Arp Up-Dn CU.A…, VALUE: AnLG LIn SUEL.
  11. Cycles: Arp Up-Dn again Cn.A…, VALUE: LOOP or 1-99 (then it rests; from key up it ends).

  2. the source: a number key, a touch of an LFO or ADSR knob, or scroll with VALUE and press Arp Assign
  3. the destination: touch its knob (ADSR knobs = envelope stages) or an LFO routing button, or scroll (names only) and press Arp Assign

MIDI

  • USB MIDI or the 5-pin MIDI IN, on any channel (SysEx over USB only), or the one set with Shift + NOISE CHnL (ALL, 1-16; in CHAn 8CH nPE the first of their channels). MIDI clock drives sync; Start lines up the bar, Continue picks up at Song Position. Or the internal clock (tap Sync Clock) or the SYNC IN jack: Shift + Sync Clock picks the source.
  • Sustain (CC 64), pitch bend (range: Settings 7), mod wheel, poly and channel aftertouch; in channel mode each channel's reach only its layer.
  • Program change 0-127 picks patch 1-128; bank select is ignored.
  • CCs: 74 cutoff, 71 resonance, 5 glide, 14 / 15 FREQ A / B, 16 FINE, 17 / 18 PW, 19 / 20 levels, 21 noise, 22 filter env amount, 23-26 filter ADSR, 73 / 75 / 70 / 72 amp A / D / S / R, 76 / 77 LFO 1, 27 / 28 LFO 2, 29 / 30 poly-mod, 31 tune, 85-90 + 102-103 Env 3 / 4.

Recovery

  • A firmware that won't start: hold 0 and 7 while switching on → bootloader; flash any .syx.
  • Retune after the synth has warmed up: Tune.

Mod matrix

Sixteen routes per layer. Each route adds source × depth to its destination, per voice. The ones marked wire are a single wire for all voices, so they follow the most recently played note. Macro 1 and Macro 2 are both destinations and sources (the M TUNE knob is Macro 1's own knob, added to its offset): route anything into a macro, then the macro into several destinations with their own depths; a macro moves once a pass (1-2.4 ms). Envelope stage destinations scale that stage's time up to 16× either way at depth 99 (sustain adds to the level). On the panel: Arp Assign, then a source (a number key, a touch of an LFO or ADSR knob, or scroll with VALUE), then a destination (touch its knob or LFO button, or scroll and press Arp Assign), VALUE for the depth; hold Arp Assign to browse the routes.

Patches

128 patches (P1-P128, program change 0-127), each with its own sequence. Saving takes the sound as it is now on the synth. Names travel with the sound; rename by typing in the name and pressing Enter (or click the loaded patch's name on the screen at the top). Export saves one patch as a 768-byte .p8p file (with its sequence), which Import writes back into any slot.

Fill the patch memory in one go (each asks first). After installing from the stock firmware the slots still hold stock-format data: format them first. The factory bank goes into the empty slots in order; the preset bank (66 sounds) into its own slots, P1-P66. "Install a bank file…" takes a bank such as the release's pro8000-presets-…json.

Play

Click the keys, or play from the computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; ' (two rows, like a DAW). Z / X shift the octave, C / V change the velocity. Keys don't play while you're typing in a text field.

MIDI routing

Forwards notes, controllers, pitch bend, aftertouch and program changes from another MIDI input (for example the Ableton Move) to the PRO 800 while this page is open. SysEx is never forwarded, so the tools' own messages stay separate.

Firmware

Tuning

Autotune measures every oscillator and filter through the tuning comparator and stores the tables in the EEPROM. It takes about 25 seconds, during which the synth is silent and doesn't answer. The Tune button on the panel does the same.


  

  

    
Knobs

    
After a patch loads, or a knob's job changes (layer, LFO, envelope, a move on this page), the
      knob is in pickup: the sound keeps the stored value until the knob reaches it or sweeps past it, and the
      display shows a dot meanwhile. With pickup off, a knob takes over as soon as you move it, and the value jumps
      to where the knob is, as on the stock synth. Letting go of Shift, MOD ASSIGN, the envelope page or Seq + FREQ
      moves nothing either way. System-wide, kept through power-off; on the synth: Shift + FIL ENV AMT
      (P.UP).
