Layers

The page follows the synth: turn a knob, press Seq1 / Seq2 or Arp Up-Dn on the panel and it shows here within a second. Moving a slider here puts that knob in pickup on the panel, just like loading a patch. The oscillator and routing buttons stay on the panel.

Parameters

Mod matrix

Eight routes per layer. Each route adds source × depth to its destination, per voice. The ones marked wire are a single wire for all voices, so they follow the most recently played note. On the panel: Arp Assign, a number key for the source (1 again swaps LFO 1 / LFO 2), touch a knob or LFO button for the destination, VALUE for the depth.

Patches

400 slots in four banks (A-D, as on the panel). Saving takes the sound as it is now on the synth. Names travel with the sound; rename by typing in the name and pressing Enter. Export saves one patch as a 256-byte .p8p file, which Import writes back into any slot.

Factory bank

The 100 sounds the PRO-800 shipped with are still in the EEPROM, untouched. Copy any of them into an empty slot. The conversion is close rather than identical, because our engine's curves differ from the stock firmware's.

Play

Click the keys, or play from the computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; ' (two rows, like a DAW). Z / X shift the octave, C / V change the velocity. Keys don't play while you're typing in a text field.

MIDI routing

Forwards notes, controllers, pitch bend, aftertouch and program changes from another MIDI input (for example the Ableton Move) to the PRO 800 while this page is open. SysEx is never forwarded, so the tools' own messages stay separate.

Tuning

Autotune measures every oscillator and filter through the tuning comparator and stores the tables in the EEPROM. It takes about 25 seconds, during which the synth is silent and doesn't answer. The Tune button on the panel does the same.
